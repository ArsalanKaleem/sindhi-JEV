Choosing between engines

Keeping, for each crop, the OCR output that v2 trusts most gives CER 0.215 and 42.3% exact words. Always using Tesseract snd gives CER 0.181 and 44.3%; a perfect chooser would reach CER 0.131. Use the scores to decide what to review, not to pick text automatically.
