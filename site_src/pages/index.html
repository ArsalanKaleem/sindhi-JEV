Sindhi-Jev

A small model that checks whether Sindhi OCR text matches the scanned image, and points to the letters whose dots look wrong. It runs on a normal CPU.

Why it exists

Sindhi letters differ by dots: ب ٻ ڀ پ, ن ڻ, ج ڄ, د ڏ ڌ. OCR engines often get the shape right and the dots wrong, and many of those mistakes are still real words, so a spell checker cannot see them. Sindhi-Jev looks at the scan and the text together and tells you which outputs to review, and which letters to look at first.

What you get

check

Probability that the OCR text is wrong, plus the suspicious letters.

repair

Tries dot variants of the suspicious letters and keeps one only if it clearly wins.

pick

Ranks several OCR outputs for the same crop, most trustworthy first.
