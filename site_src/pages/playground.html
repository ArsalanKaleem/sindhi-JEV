Scores how likely the text is wrong and which letters look suspicious.

Reading the result

  • Percentage: chance the text does not match the crop. Treat it as a ranking score (see Limitations).
  • Letter tiles: darker red means more suspicious; a red outline means flagged (score above 0.817).
  • Scan and render: the render is the text drawn the way the model sees it. Differences in dots between the two are what the model looks for.

Run it locally

The playground needs the checker service. On your own computer:

python tools/demo/server.py

Then open http://localhost:8770/playground.html. Images stay on your computer.
