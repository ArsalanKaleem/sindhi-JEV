Limitations

Read this before relying on the scores.

Word crops only

The model was trained on crops of 1 to 4 words. On real full lines it is at chance (AUROC 0.458). Cut lines into words first.

Printed Naskh-style text only

Training used 9 printed Naskh-style fonts with simulated scan damage. Handwriting, Nastaliq and decorative display type are untested; expect poor results.

The percentage is a ranking score

p_wrong is temperature-scaled on synthetic data. On real words its calibration error (ECE) is 0.118, so a value of 0.80 does not mean exactly 80%. Sorting by it works well: reviewing the 30% most suspicious outputs catches about 39% of the wrong ones, against 30% for random review. Most real outputs in the test set are wrong (74%), which limits how much any ranking can gain here.

Letter flags are a hint

The top flagged letter is right 84% of the time on wrong outputs, but the full set of flags has F1 0.47. Look at the top flag first.

It does not choose text better than the best engine

pick ranks candidates by the model's score. On real words this did not beat always using Tesseract snd (CER 0.215 vs 0.181).

Repair is measured on synthetic data only

repair fixed 98% of real-word dot swaps in training fonts and 87% in unseen fonts, breaking 1 to 2 correct texts in 2,000. It has not been measured on real scans.

Encoding variants are not errors

ه / ھ / ہ, ي / ی / ى and ڪ / ک / ك code-point differences are reported separately and do not count as errors in the results.

Batching

The model's CNN uses batch normalisation. In a padded batch, padding becomes non-zero after normalisation and leaks into the last real tokens, which is the start of a right-to-left word. The tool therefore scores one crop per forward pass. All reported results were computed in batches of 64; on the synthetic test sets this changes AUROC by at most 0.0001.

Test-set size

The real set has 336 word crops and 74 line crops from 14 sources. Confidence intervals are wide for small subsets (for example the 239 real-word errors), and the line result rests on 17 correct outputs.
