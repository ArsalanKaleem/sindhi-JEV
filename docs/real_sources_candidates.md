# Candidate sources for real Sindhi scans (leads, NOT yet verified)

Nothing here has been downloaded. Before any item is used: open its page, confirm
(1) script is Perso-Arabic Sindhi (Indian Sindhi books may be in Devanagari),
(2) the rights statement, then log it in `data/real/sources.csv`.

| Lead | What | Rights (as claimed, unverified) | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| PG Sindhi Library (PDFs on archive.org), described by Digital Orientalist, 2023 and 2025 | Post-Partition Sindhi books from India, >1,000 received for digitisation | Blog calls them public domain; check each archive.org item | printed stratum, main source if rights hold |
| Digital Library of India items on archive.org, e.g. `in.ernet.dli.2015.458518` ("History Of Sindhi Literature", Ajwani) | Older scans, often poor quality (good for realism) | Marked "Public Domain" by DLI; DLI marks are known to be unreliable, so check author death year (Pakistan/India: life + 50/60 years) | printed stratum |
| Wikimedia Commons, Category:Sindhi language and subcategories | A few images and documents | per-file license on Commons (usually CC/PD) | printed stratum, small |
| Sindhi Language Authority e-library (sindhila.edu.pk), Sindh Culture e-library (el.sindhculture.gov.pk) | Large Sindhi book collections, read or download online | Copyrighted unless stated; ask permission | eval-only after permission; never redistribute |
| Sindhi newspapers' e-paper (Kawish, Awami Awaz, Ibrat, …) | Newsprint, low quality, ads | Copyrighted | eval only; release URL + bbox + gold text only |
| Your own handwriting (+ consenting volunteers) | Write new sentences, not from Wikipedia | yours (CC BY 4.0) | handwriting stratum |
| Your own phone photos of printed material you own | Realistic camera noise | photo yours, text may be copyrighted | eval only; release gold text only if allowed |

Plan: about 15–25 sources × up to 40 crops each, across 1960s–2020s print and both
Pakistani and Indian publishers, to keep fonts and print quality varied.
